# Ledger — shared, append-only

> One entry per meaningful moment, hard *and* good (the good ones teach as much). Newest first. Either person can write. Both can add their side of the **same** moment; that's where real understanding happens. Mark an entry `private-to: <name>` to keep it from the other person.

**Honest, not tidy.** "I lost my patience and it escalated" is as valuable as a win.

## Entry format
```
### YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM — short title  (by: NAME)
- **Context:** where, when, what was going on (tired? transition? new place?)
- **What I meant / did:**
- **What I think they took from it:**
- **Their side** (added by them, if they want): what they heard / felt
- **What helped / what to adjust:**
- **Tags:** e.g. transition, bedtime, sensory-sound, new-place, win, repair, misread
```
