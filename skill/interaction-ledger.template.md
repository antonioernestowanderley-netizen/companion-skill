# Interaction ledger

> Copy this to `interaction-ledger.md`. Append-only. One entry per meaningful moment — hard ones *and* good ones (the good ones teach just as much). Newest at the top. Over time this becomes the companion's real understanding of this person, in their own track record rather than in theory.

**Keep it honest, not tidy.** "I lost my patience and it escalated" is as valuable a data point as a win.

---

## Entry format
```
### YYYY-MM-DD — short title
- **Context:** where, when, what was going on (tired? transition? new place?)
- **Their state:** how they seemed; any early-warning cues you noticed
- **What I tried:** what you said/did
- **Asked → answered:** each question exactly as asked → ✓ / ~ / ✗ + what they said (e.g. `"Did Leo come to class?" → ✓ "yes, he sat with me"`)
- **How they responded:** what actually happened
- **What worked / what I'd adjust:** the lesson for next time
- **Tags:** e.g. transition, bedtime, sensory-sound, new-place, win, repair
```

The companion writes these with `bin/log-moment.sh`, including `### … — review` markers after each review of the Question bank.
